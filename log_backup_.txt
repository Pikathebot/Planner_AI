# Plan: Baking a Cake from Scratch

## Overview
This plan provides a step-by-step guide to baking a cake from scratch. It covers the essential ingredients, preparation, and baking process to produce a delicious homemade cake.

## Prerequisites
| Requirement | Why It's Needed |
|---|---|
| Basic kitchen tools (mixing bowls, measuring cups, etc.) | To prepare and mix ingredients safely and efficiently |
| Room-temperature butter | For optimal creaming with sugar |

## Learning Path
1. **Understanding Cake Ingredients** — learning about the basic components of a cake recipe
2. **Creaming Butter and Sugar** — mastering the technique for incorporating air into the mixture
3. **Mixing Wet and Dry Ingredients** — combining wet and dry ingredients to form a batter
4. **Baking the Cake** — understanding the baking process and checking for doneness

## Step-by-step Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (30 minutes)
- [ ] Learn about basic cake ingredients, including flour, sugar, eggs, butter, and baking powder.
- [ ] Understand the importance of room-temperature butter for creaming.

### Phase 2 — Setup & Preparation (20 minutes)
- [ ] Preheat the oven to the recommended temperature (350°F/175°C).
- [ ] Prepare a 9-inch round cake pan by greasing it with butter or cooking spray.
- [ ] Measure and sift dry ingredients (flour, baking powder) into a separate bowl.

### Phase 3 — Execution (40 minutes)
- [ ] Cream room-temperature butter and sugar until just combined (about 2 minutes).
- [ ] Add eggs one at a time, mixing until well incorporated.
- [ ] Gradually add dry ingredients to the wet mixture, alternating with milk or buttermilk.
- [ ] Mix just until combined, stopping to scrape down sides if needed.

### Phase 4 — Launch / Completion (30 minutes)
- [ ] Spread cake batter into the prepared pan.
- [ ] Bake cake in the preheated oven for 30 minutes, checking for doneness at the 20-minute mark.
- [ ] Remove from oven and let cool in the pan for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack.

## Estimated Timeline
| Phase | Estimated Duration |
|---|---|
| Phase 1 — Foundation | 30 minutes |
| Phase 2 — Setup | 20 minutes |
| Phase 3 — Execution | 40 minutes |
| Phase 4 — Launch | 30 minutes |
